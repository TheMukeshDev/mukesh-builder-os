01 — About

A builder with a bias for shipping.

I'm Mukesh Kumar, a second-year B.Tech Computer Science student at BBSCET, Prayagraj. My work sits where backend engineering meets AI: Python and Flask on the server, AI models and automation on top, and a working product at the end.

I care about the unglamorous parts — clean API design, validation, structured data, and systems that hold up when real people use them. That focus shows up across an AI codebase navigator, an AI event planner, and a metrology testing workflow.

Outside the editor, I build developer community. As Community Lead at Tech Hub BBS and Team Lead at NEXORA E-Cell, I organize events, coordinate teams, and help students find opportunities. I'm currently going deeper into C++, DSA, React, and the MERN stack.
